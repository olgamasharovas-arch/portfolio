Pigeon Mountain Industries

Pigeon Mountain Industries is a U.S.-based manufacturer of professional rope and vertical safety equipment, with over 40 years of experience.
I led a full rebranding project, which included designing a new logo that reflects the company’s evolution while preserving the legacy of the original mark in a more minimal, modern form. A complete brand book was developed to ensure consistency across all touchpoints.
The new style was applied across various platforms:
● Website redesign, with a strong focus on improving the product catalog and checkout experience
● Full print catalog redesign, including new product tables and visual structure
● Marketing materials: magazine ads, roll-up banners, social media visuals, digital banners
● Article and blog design for the website
● Created visual ideas for trade show presence
● Executed layout and development on Tilda, basic motion graphics and edits when needed
